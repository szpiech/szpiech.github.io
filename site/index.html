Welcome to the Szpiech Lab

We are a computational biology lab in the Department of Biology at Penn State University in State College, PA. We are interested in how population history, inbreeding, and natural selection shape the distribution of genetic variation within and between populations. To study these processes, we use mathematical modeling, simulations, and large-scale data analysis. We also develop statistical methods and software for analyzing genomic data. Our work has relevance to medical genetics, anthropology, and conservation genetics. In addition to human data, we work with data from rhesus macaques, dogs, dingoes, and North American songbirds, often in collaboration with other labs.
